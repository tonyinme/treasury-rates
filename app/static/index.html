What the markers mean

AuctionOfficial Treasury issuance result. The date is the Treasury auction date and the price is the auction price.

Market estimatePrior-business-day Treasury rate. Price is modeled from the official daily yield, coupon, and maturity—not a live bid or ask.

Treasury bills have no coupon. Their market estimates use Treasury’s daily coupon-equivalent bill yields; note and bond estimates use the daily par yield curve.
